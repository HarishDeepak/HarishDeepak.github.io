Driver monitoring test benchoct 2026
A camera-based driver monitor, tested against Euro NCAP's warning-timing rules and wired into CARLA.

on-time warnings on real drivers went from 7/66 to 22/49, mostly by fixing the test rather than the detector. Foundation models (DINOv2, CLIP) matched a simple eyelid measure but didn't beat it.

PragmaBot on a Franka FR32026
A frozen vision-language model that plans, checks its work and learns from failures through memory, on a real arm.

The paper's code stops at planning; this added segmentation, grasp synthesis and a ROS 2 skill layer. Stereo depth artefacts cut from 17.7 to 7.3 cm; with short-term memory the robot recovered from a failed plan in 3/5 runs.

Cube stacking on a Franka Panda, then ROS 22025 – 26
Text-prompted segmentation, cubes fitted in 3D, MoveIt stacks a six-cube tower. Later ported to ROS 2 Humble.

Six-cube tower on the real robot. The ROS 2 port runs simulation, control and planning; perception and stacking are ported but not wired together yet.

Diffusion policy + LoRAjul 2026
LeRobot's diffusion policy on PushT: evaluation harness, two silent bugs fixed, LoRA fine-tuning.

Success rate 5/10 → 5/10 after LoRA. The interesting parts were a checkpoint that silently dropped its normalization and a data loader that ate more than 100 GB of RAM.

Open-vocabulary aerial segmentationsummer 2026
Three text-prompted segmentation methods compared on ISPRS Potsdam, with Fraunhofer IGD.

SegEarth-OV3 (SAM 3) reached 70.6% mIoU zero-shot on six tiles, against 64.4% for RSKT-Seg with a sliding window. A prompt and threshold study on Hessen orthophotos followed.
